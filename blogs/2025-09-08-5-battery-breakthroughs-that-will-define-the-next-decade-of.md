World EV Day celebrates the rapid progress of electric vehicles and the batteries that power them. In just a couple of decades, we’ve gone from early EVs powered by modified laptop batteries to batteries powerful enough to electrify trucks, planes, trains and more. While the journey has included bumps and growing pains, the road ahead is even more exciting.
