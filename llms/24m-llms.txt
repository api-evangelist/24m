# 24M Technologies

> 24M Technologies is a battery technology company founded in 2010 out of MIT laboratories and headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts. It licenses battery materials, cell architecture and its SemiSolid manufacturing platform to energy-storage system integrators, EV battery manufacturers and OEMs rather than manufacturing cells at scale itself. 24M is a hardware, materials and manufacturing company and publishes no public developer API, SDK, or machine-readable API contract.

## Products

- [Eternalyte Electrolyte](https://24-m.com/eternalyte): Electrolyte for ultra-fast charging and low-temperature performance.
- [Impervio Separator](https://24-m.com/impervio): Separator technology aimed at preventing thermal runaway.
- [LiForever Electrode](https://24-m.com/liforever): Electrode technology targeting cell longevity.
- [24M ETOP](https://24-m.com/etop): Electrode-to-pack architecture for pack-level energy density.
- [Production Equipment](https://24-m.com/production-equipment): Manufacturing equipment for SemiSolid cell production.
